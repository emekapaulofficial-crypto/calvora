Terms and privacy

How you can use this site and how we handle your information.

Terms of use

By using this website you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the site.

What this site is

Calvora Property Solutions (“Calvora”, “we”) runs an online platform where registered agents advertise homes for rent, homes for sale and land for sale. Agents work with Calvora as independent partners. We check properties before they are shown as verified, but you should still inspect a property and read any agreement before you pay.

Listings and verification

  • A property is described as verified only when the “Verification Completed” label is shown, meaning all four Calvora checks are on record.
  • An agent badge describes the agent’s track record. It is not a statement about any property.
  • Prices, fees and availability can change. Listings are re-confirmed regularly, and one that has not been re-confirmed shows “Availability Confirmation Pending”.

Payments

  • Pay only by bank transfer to the official Calvora account shown on this website.
  • Do not pay into personal accounts. Calvora is not responsible for money paid to anyone else.
  • We disclose service charges before payment and never require immediate payment.

Your responsibilities

Give accurate information in enquiries, reviews and complaints. Do not post false reviews, impersonate others or misuse the site.

Reviews

Reviews are checked by our staff before they appear. We may remove a review that is false, abusive or cannot be verified.

Limits

We work hard to keep information accurate but cannot promise it is always complete or error-free. To the extent the law allows, Calvora is not liable for losses from your reliance on a listing without inspecting the property or from payments made outside our official channels.

Complaints

Use our complaints page. Every complaint gets a reference number.

Privacy notice

We take the privacy of customers, owners and agents seriously and aim to follow Nigerian data protection law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What we collect

  • Enquiries, inspection requests and complaints: your name, phone number, optional email and your message.
  • Reviews: your name, phone number (used only to verify the review) and your comment. Only your first name is shown publicly.
  • Agent applications: personal details, contact details and identity documents, kept private and seen only by authorised staff.
  • Property owners: owner details and proof of authority, seen only by the listing agent and authorised staff.

How we use it

To respond to you, arrange inspections, verify properties and agents, investigate complaints, keep records of our operations and keep the platform safe.

Who can see it

Your enquiry is seen by the agent for that property and authorised Calvora staff. We never show phone numbers, home addresses or identity documents on public pages, including ID verification pages. We do not sell personal information.

Keeping it safe

Access is restricted by role, and private records are stored securely with our service provider.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how we use it. Contact us and we will respond.

Changes

We will update this notice here when anything changes.
