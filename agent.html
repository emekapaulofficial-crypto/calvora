Build your property business with a name people trust

Calvora agent partners get a professional listing platform, a personal dashboard, real customer enquiries and clear, traceable commission. You work independently, with our support and standards.

What you get

  • A public profile with your photo, ID number and badge
  • A dashboard for your listings, enquiries, inspections and commission
  • A printable ID card with a QR code customers can scan
  • A badge that grows with your record: Bronze, Silver, Gold and Diamond

How you join

  1. Create an account and apply

    Send your details, your ID document and your agreement to our code of conduct.

  2. We review

    Our operations team checks your application and may ask for more information.

  3. Get your ID

    Once approved you receive your agent ID number and your dashboard opens.

  4. List and earn

    Add properties. Each one is checked by our team before it goes live.

What we expect

No fake listings, no hidden fees, no pressure on customers, and no taking money outside official channels. Read the full code of conduct.
