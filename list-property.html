List your property

We check it, advertise it and handle the enquiries.

Reach people looking for property, without the noise

Own a house or land in Ado-Ekiti? We list it through a registered Calvora agent, check it, and handle the enquiries and inspections, so you do not have to answer every call yourself.

What we need from you

  • Your name and phone number
  • Proof that you own the property or are allowed to let or sell it
  • Your written permission for us to advertise it
  • The price and any fees you expect the customer to pay
  • Clear, honest photographs of the real property

How it works

  1. Contact us

    Message us or speak to a Calvora agent.

  2. We visit and check

    Our team views the property and confirms your permission and the details.

  3. It goes live

    Once the checks are recorded, it appears in the right catalogue.

  4. We keep it current

    We re-confirm availability regularly so customers are not misled.

Ask us about any fee before you agree to anything. Any charge will be explained in writing first, and a Calvora agent will never collect rent or deposits for you without authority.
