A property is called verified only after four checks

  1. The agent’s identity is checked
  2. Our team views the property
  3. The owner’s permission to advertise is checked
  4. The details are recently confirmed with the owner

Each check is recorded with evidence. If it is not on record, we do not say it.

Verified means something here

Agent verification and property verification are two different things, and we never mix them up.

Property label. “Verification Completed” appears only when all four checks are recorded in our system.

Agent badge. Bronze, Silver, Gold and Diamond are earned from real listings, completed deals and customer ratings, never from how many photos an agent uploads.

How we keep you safe
  • Every agent is registered and has an ID number you can check on our website
  • No property goes live until our team has recorded the owner’s permission
  • Fees are shown on the listing and explained before you pay anything
  • Payments go by bank transfer to the official company account only
  • Complaints get a reference number and a written outcome

How it works

  1. Browse

    Search by area and price. Look at the costs and the labels on each property.

  2. Ask or book

    Send an enquiry or book an inspection. Your request is saved, so it does not get lost.

  3. Visit with an agent

    A registered Calvora agent shows you the property. Check their ID on this site first.

  4. Decide and pay safely

    If you go ahead, pay by bank transfer to the official account and keep the receipt.
