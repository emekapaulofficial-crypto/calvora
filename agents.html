How badges are earned

Badges are worked out automatically from our own records. Nobody can type one in, and uploading lots of listings is not enough on its own.

Only reviews that our staff have verified count towards an agent’s rating. Suspended or expired agents lose their badge. These starting levels will be reviewed after the first few months.
